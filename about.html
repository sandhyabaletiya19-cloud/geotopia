Our Story

Born from a Love of
Geography

DharaVerse was created in 2026 by Sandhya Baletiya — a passionate geography enthusiast who believed that our planet deserved better than dry textbooks and lifeless maps.

The vision was simple but powerful: build an interactive geography platform that makes every student, every curious mind, and every explorer feel the wonder of our world — not just memorize it.

What started as a personal project quickly grew into one of the most comprehensive geography learning platforms available online — covering everything from the highest mountain peaks to the deepest ocean trenches, from ancient geological timelines to modern geopolitical maps.

Our Mission

Geography is Not Just a Subject.
It is How We Understand Our World.

At DharaVerse we believe that understanding geography means understanding everything — why rivers shaped civilizations, why mountains divided nations, why oceans connected peoples separated by thousands of miles. Our mission is to tell that story with the depth, accuracy and visual richness it deserves. For every learner. On every device. In every language.

✦ Creator & Developer

Sandhya Baletiya

Founder of DharaVerse

Sandhya is a passionate geography enthusiast and developer who built DharaVerse from scratch with one goal in mind: to create the geography platform she always wished existed. Every feature, every dataset, every interactive tool on this platform reflects her belief that geography should inspire wonder — not induce boredom.

From coding the interactive 3D globe to writing the geography encyclopedia, from designing the UPSC modules to building 25+ geography games — DharaVerse is a labor of love for our planet.

DharaVerse covers 195 countries, all 7 continents, Earth's major mountain ranges, river systems, ocean basins, desert landscapes, tropical forests, volcanic regions, island chains and coral reef ecosystems. Every piece of content is researched, written and verified to ensure accuracy — because geography matters and getting it right matters even more. Whether you are a student in Mumbai, a teacher in London or a curious explorer in São Paulo, DharaVerse was built for you.
