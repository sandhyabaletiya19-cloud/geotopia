About DharaVerse

Who We Are

DharaVerse is an interactive geography learning platform created in 2026 by Sandhya Baletiya. Our mission is simple: to make world geography accessible, engaging, and deeply meaningful for every learner on the planet — from age 6 to 96.

Our Story

DharaVerse was born from a singular vision — geography should never be boring. Traditional textbooks present our planet as a collection of names, borders, and capital cities to memorize. We believed there was a better way.

Today DharaVerse stands as one of the most comprehensive geography education platforms available online, covering 195 countries, all 7 continents, Earth's 4.5 billion year geological timeline, and an ever growing encyclopedia of geographic wonders.

What We Offer

  • Interactive 3D Globe with 195 countries
  • Geography Encyclopedia covering mountains, rivers, oceans, forests, deserts, islands, lakes, volcanoes and coral reefs
  • 25+ Geography Games for all age groups
  • UPSC Geography Preparation Module
  • Earth Timeline covering 4.5 billion years
  • World Atlas with interactive maps
  • Kids Zone, Junior Zone and Teen Zone

Our Mission

We believe that understanding geography means understanding our world. Every mountain range, every river, every ocean current tells a story about how our planet works and how human civilization evolved. At DharaVerse those stories are told with depth, accuracy and visual richness they deserve.

Who Created DharaVerse

DharaVerse was created by Sandhya Baletiya, a passionate geography enthusiast and developer who wanted to build the geography platform she always wished existed. What started as a personal project quickly grew into a comprehensive platform serving geography learners across India and around the world.
